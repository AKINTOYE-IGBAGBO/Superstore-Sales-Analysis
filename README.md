# Superstore-Sales-Analysis

To analyze company's growth and investigate the factors or behaviors contributing to the growth using interactive reports.

[RAW DATA](https://github.com/AKINTOYE-IGBAGBO/Superstore-Sales-Analysis/blob/main/Sample%20-%20Superstore.csv)

---

### Objectives

1. Analyze overall sales performance of the company.
2. Investigate which regions generate the highest sales and profit.
3. Investigate which customer segments contribute the most revenue.
4. Investigate which product categories perform best and are profitable.
5. Identify Sales trends over time.
6. Provide recommendations management should implement to improve business performance.

### Insights 

**Key Business Insights**

1.	A slow growth was observed from 2014 to 2015 while a sharp growth from 2016 maintaining above 20 percent growth. The company increased in sales over the year from 2014 to 2017 with 52 % growth in sales indicating a good sales performance overall.	

2.	The company generated the most revenue and profit from the west region.

3.	Technology category of products generated the highest sales.

4.	Sales peaked in the month of November while the least sales occur in February.

5.	Highest sales were generated from consumers segment of customers could mean majority of the customers are consumers.

6.	Sales performance are unstable through out the year as there are extreme lows ang high.

**Business Risk**

1.	Some products are sold at a loss which is not sustainable for the business and create overdependence on the profitable ones.

2.	Home office customers sale is very low compared to other segment and risk exposing the business to overdependence of other segments.

3.	The Central region having the least profitable exposes the business to overdependence on other region taking into consideration expenses of running the business.

**Opportunities**

1.	Average sales made towards from September to December is higher and resources could be pulled towards that shift to maximize sales and profit.

2.	Profitable products subcategory like Copiers and Accessories in the technology category with low investment and high profit turn up rate is an opportunity to achieve maximum impact, while also increasing the standard for other sub-categories that are profitable.

3.	The East region as a second-growing region provides an opportunity to increase sales and maximize profit.
